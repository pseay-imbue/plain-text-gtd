# Weekly Review

Walk me through the weekly review. Go slowly; the point is to surface things
I am avoiding, not to rush. Read `data/.apps/gtd/instructions.md` first so we are
working from my current triage rules.

## 1. Get clear

- Ask me whether I have any loose paper, screenshots, or pinned tabs to dump
  into Inbox before we start. Pause until I confirm we're clear.
- Read through my Inbox with me one item at a time. Apply the triage flow in
  `instructions.md`. Leave vague items in Inbox flagged for deeper thinking
  unless I want to think them through right now.

## 2. Get current

- **Recently done** — list items I've marked `done` in the last week. Ask if
  any closed prematurely or are actually still open.
- **Calendar (past two weeks)** — anything that happened that generated new
  next actions? Capture them.
- **Calendar (next two weeks)** — anything that needs preparation? Capture
  prep items.
- **Next Actions** — read them out, group by context. For each, ask: still
  the next physical step? Still relevant? Mark stale ones for re-triage.
- **Waiting For** — for each, when did I last hear? Should I nudge?
- **Projects** — one at a time, read the project title and its children.
  Confirm there is at least one Next Action or sub-project. If a project has
  gone quiet, ask whether to drop it to `someday`.

## 3. Get creative

- **Someday/Maybe** — read through. Anything I want to promote to a project
  now? Anything to delete?
- **Read/Review** — anything I want to consume this week?
- Open prompt: is there anything I'm avoiding that we haven't surfaced yet?
  Anything new I want to start?

## 4. Close out

- Summarize: what changed during the review (new projects, dropped items,
  promotions, demotions). Don't write a long retrospective — just the deltas.
