Getting started

The fastest way to fill your system is one big dump — no need to file items one at a time to start.

  1. Dump your whole list. Paste everything on your mind into the box below — one item per line — and hit File.
  2. Triage all in chat. A Triage all in chat button appears once you've filed items. Click it and your agent sorts everything into Next Actions, Projects, Waiting, and more.

After that you can add or refine items one at a time whenever you like.
